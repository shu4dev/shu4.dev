I'm Brian Shu, an M.S. student in Computer Science at Purdue University, working in the Cognitive Robot Autonomy and Learning (CoRAL) Lab with Prof. Ahmed H. Qureshi. My research interests are robot learning, dexterous manipulation, imitation learning and simulation benchmarks.

Right now I am developing a general-purpose robot manipulation policy for [dishwasher tasks](/projects/dishwasher/). At the Hawaiʻi AI Initiative (HawAII), University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, I co-developed [Fiatlux](/projects/fiatlux/), an Isaac Lab benchmark in which a Unitree G1 humanoid climbs a ladder to replace a bulb, now under review at IEEE ICRA 2027, and upgraded a privileged expert for UR5e [cable insertion](/projects/cable-insertion/) with force-monitored descent and jam recovery.

I'm looking for a robotics research internship for Summer 2027. If your lab or team works on robot learning, manipulation or simulation benchmarks, I would like to hear from you.
