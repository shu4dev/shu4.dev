I'm Brian Shu, an M.S. student in Computer Science at Purdue University, working in the Cognitive Robot Autonomy & Learning (CoRAL) Lab with Prof. Ahmed H. Qureshi. I work on robot learning for long-horizon manipulation: imitation learning, post-training vision-language-action (VLA) models, and the benchmarks that tell you whether a policy actually works.

Right now that is a [dishwasher-loading policy](/projects/dishwasher/) in NVIDIA Isaac Lab. Before Purdue I built the subtask environments and asset pipeline for [Fiatlux](/projects/fiatlux/), a humanoid ladder-climbing benchmark under review at ICRA 2027, and a force-monitored expert for [cable insertion](/projects/cable-insertion/) on a UR5.

I'm looking for a robotics research internship for Summer 2027 and can work full-time from May 10 to August 20, 2027. If your lab or team works on manipulation, imitation learning or evaluation, I would like to hear from you.
